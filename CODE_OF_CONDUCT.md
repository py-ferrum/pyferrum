# Code of Conduct

Participation in pyferrum is expected to be respectful and constructive.
Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, threats, and publishing another person's private information
without permission.

Project participants should:

- Discuss technical disagreements without attacking people.
- Give useful, specific feedback and be open to good-faith correction.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Keep project spaces focused on pyferrum and its contributors.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository
maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not post personal details in public
issues. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when
necessary to keep the project safe and welcoming.
